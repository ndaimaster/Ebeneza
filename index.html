About Us

Every child deserves a chance to shine

Ebeneza Foundation works with vulnerable children, street children and orphans in Tanzania. Our programs focus on safe reintegration with families, access to education, vocational skills, psychosocial support and the distribution of food and essential items.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A community where every vulnerable child is safe, supported and able to build a hopeful future.

Our Mission

To walk alongside street children, orphans and vulnerable families in Tanzania — through counseling, family tracing and reintegration, education support, vocational training, life skills and the provision of food and basic material assistance.

Our Services

Practical programs designed to protect, educate and empower vulnerable children and their families.

Child Protection & Family Reintegration

Safely tracing families and transporting displaced children back to stable, protective homes, with follow-up support.

Education Support

Helping vulnerable children access and stay in school through fees, materials and mentoring support.

Vocational Skills Development

Practical skills and readiness programs that give older beneficiaries a path to earning a living.

Food & Clothing Assistance

Regular distribution of meals, clothing and basic supplies to children and families in need.
